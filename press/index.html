Description

Monsters pour down from the sky, and your hero shoots them automatically. Your only job is to make that hero stronger.

Spend gold to level up your hero, unlock skills that sweep the screen in different ways, and summon mercenaries from a gacha — pick two to fight alongside you. A duplicate pull is simply a miss, which is where the "luck" in the title comes from.

Every stage brings new art and new monsters, from Meadow Village through Lava Fields and the Abyssal Labyrinth to the Ancient Battlefield. Every tenth stage, a giant boss appears alone after the waves are cleared; bring it down before it reaches your wall for a special reward. Clear the main stages and an endless mode opens up.

Progress saves to the cloud automatically, and a combat-power leaderboard lets you compare with other players. Watching ads is optional — every stage can be cleared without them.
